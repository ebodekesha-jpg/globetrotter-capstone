● About this project

Built by the community, for the community.

Yaoundé Community Explorer is a neighbor-powered guide to the city's markets, hills, museums and gathering places — no travel agency, no ads, just honest local knowledge.

Why it exists

Yaoundé rewards the people who explore it.

Spread across seven hills, the city hides its best spots in plain sight — a sunrise view, a Sunday lakeside stroll, a workshop where artisans still cast bronze by hand. This project collects those spots in one place, mapped with OpenStreetMap and rated by the people who actually visit them.
